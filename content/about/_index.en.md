This blog has been active since 2005.

The original idea was to write about the art of building software; over time, other topics crept in. As of this writing (January 2020), the blog holds more than a thousand articles.

Over the years I focused more and more on software engineering, and that has been the main subject of this blog for a long time now.

That is why, alongside this blog, I keep two other sites (both in Spanish):

- [La Sombra de Dijkstra](https://programando.org/), where I write exclusively about programming.
- [Akarrú](https://akarru.com/), where I publish about my hobbies and personal interests.

Since 2020 all the content of this blog has been available in this GitHub repository: <https://github.com/lnds/lnds-site>. If you find a typo, I would appreciate a pull request fixing it.

My name is Eduardo Díaz. I hold a degree in computer engineering and a master's in information technology from the Universidad de Chile. I have been an entrepreneur and an executive at several companies in Chile. I am a speaker and a writer.

My book "La Naturaleza del Software" is available on Amazon at this URL: <https://amzn.to/2RcA9W6>

**A note on the English version.** The blog is written in Spanish. Posts from August 2025 onward are also available in English; the rest of the archive, twenty years of it, is only in [Spanish](/).
