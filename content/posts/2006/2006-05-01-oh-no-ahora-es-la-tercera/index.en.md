I've been reading [Guaquil](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://www.blogmemes.com/comment.php?meme_id=1885), who [writes on his blog about the third iteration of the website of La Tercera](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://rguaiquil.wordpress.com/2006/04/30/la-tercera-estrena-su-papel-digital/), the Chilean daily. I congratulate them, and I want to make clear that my criticism isn't personal, since I don't know them, nor is it aimed at their professional quality, which I consider remarkable, given that La Tercera's site is a good site. But I'm afraid functional decisions have been made that hurt its usability.

I stopped using [La Tercera](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://www.latercera.cl/) on the web a long time ago, and that's with journalist friends who work at the paper. To read it I prefer the print edition.

Notice I said use, not read, because websites are used. You have a _user experience_ with a website; websites aren't read, they're used, I insist.

I have the honor of being a friend of [Pepe Flores](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://www.levtraru.com/), the innovative creator of the first version of La Tercera's website, which I liked a lot.

But afterwards it seems more thought went into _technological cool stuff_ than into the _value of information_, and La Tercera lost interest in its **users**.

The design may be very pretty, fast and well thought out, and the so-called digital paper may be the latest trend in site design, but the reality is that **what matters is the news, not the graphic design**.

At the very least, the changes this site has gone through have cost me the following:

  1. They force me to subscribe to read past editions, which I don't want to do, because I don't want to give them my RUT (national ID number) or tell them where I live, and all that just to be able to go back 7 days in the news

	
  2. They removed everything before 2001

	
  3. I can no longer read an interview they did with me about the adoption of AES, so I could show it to my children and future grandchildren.

	
  4. I was never able to sync my AvantGo again

	
  5. The WAP version is gone.


OK, AvantGo and WAP may be _démodé_, but they're useful, and they still are.

But worst of all, Google no longer works with La Tercera.

And now the news has to be read in FLASH!! At least [LUN ](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://www.lun.com/)went with a standard technology :)


> While I was writing this post, I was trying to [view a news story ](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080946/http://latercera.codisa.cl/ltrep/edicionparam.html?20060430,32,8)in "digital paper", and you know what? The Flash logo is still spinning, and I suspect I won't be able to read it, and I have 1 Mb broadband.


As you can see, **there is no such thing as progress**.
