There is a very telling sequence in the documentary Revolution OS. Stallman is "thanking" the audience for the first "Linus Torvalds Award", and he delivers one of his clever lines: it's "like giving the Han Solo award to the Rebel Alliance."

This whole controversy is a waste of energy.

Stallman has insisted on talking about "GNU plus Linux". I think it's better to say Linux plus GNU, the same way we can say Linux plus Apache; we even have the famous acronym: LAMP (Linux Apache Mysql PHP).

The truth is that this discussion is somewhat byzantine, although very revealing. There is a divide, a deep one. When you keep reading [the thread on the linux kernel list](http://lkml.org/lkml/2006/9/27/191), you run into another one of those blunt Torvalds replies. For Linus this is an almost epistemological discussion: to him the FSF people hold a religious position, but he wants to do science.
