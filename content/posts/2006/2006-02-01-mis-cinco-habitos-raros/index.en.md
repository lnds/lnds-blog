As if crashing my car hadn't been enough, now I've been infected with this meme by [Carlos](http://web.archive.org/web/20080410095518/http://eldiabloenlosdetalles.net/).

Well, here are my 5 weird habits:



	
  1. **Buying notebooks and planners and never using them.** I'm always buying some notebook to take notes in, and I never get past the third page. In the end my kids, or my wife, end up using them.

	
  2. **Programming on Tritón cookies and coffee.** They're the best for staying focused :) and the worst for heartburn :(

	
  3. **Buying books.** I can't walk past a bookstore without buying a book, so I'm a compulsive buyer, which has led to several piles of books spread around my house.

	
  4. **I can't stand ties or dressing formally**. Fortunately clients aren't so strict anymore, but I hate having to go to a meeting in a tie. Mandatory tie-wearing should be considered a human rights violation.

	
  5. **Eating a good lomo tomate mayo once a month** (a pork loin sandwich with tomato and mayo), preferably at the Fuente Alemana. A ritual not to be missed (but don't tell my wife).


And since nobody else gets off the hook, I'm infecting [Ricardo](http://web.archive.org/web/20080410095518/http://www.ricardodiaz.org/), [Jens](http://web.archive.org/web/20080410095518/http://www.hardings.cl/blog/), [Shezzo ](http://web.archive.org/web/20080410095518/http://www.nerdpride.org/blog/)and [Hackand](http://web.archive.org/web/20080410095518/http://www.hackand.cl/).
