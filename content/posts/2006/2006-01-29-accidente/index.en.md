**Your life can change in a second. One moment you're driving down the highway and the next you're flipped over, not really understanding what happened.**

Yesterday I had a car accident. Fortunately I was alone and nobody got hurt. I only got a couple of scratches and I'm a little sore.

You'll understand that this will keep me offline for a while. You have to reflect on these things, why they happen, and learn from them. For now I need to rest.

I could have died, simple as that.  
I don't feel like writing about anything, [or arguing about licenses or free software with anyone](http://mnm.uib.es/gallir/posts/2006/01/28/610/). For now, I just want to be with my family and rest.

Take care, and always remember that someone is waiting for you at home.
