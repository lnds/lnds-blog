In life there are no problems, only solutions we haven't found yet.

Having problems is a way of getting better every day. Dare to face them!

Emergency Principle: if you keep calm while everyone else is losing their heads, you clearly haven't grasped the problem.

I know of no better motivation than problems.

The problems don't matter, the solution does.

The solution is in the problem.

If the problem has a solution, worrying is no use, and if the problem has no solution, worrying is no use.

In this life you have to be a solution, not a problem.

We build our problems ourselves.

If you have a problem and you can solve it, don't worry, solve it.
If you have a problem and you can't solve it, don't worry either, forget it.

Problems only get solved by having something to think about.

One thing is clear: there are more problemologists than solutionologists.

If you have problems, remember that it could always be worse.

Every problem has a solution; if it doesn't, it's not a problem...

That's the typical nonsense from someone who has never had a serious problem.

People don't understand that problems are, and will be, the reason to live tomorrow.

When all is lost, it's time to go for broke.

Don't talk about your problems unless it's to find them a solution.

If a problem has a solution, why worry? If it doesn't, why worry?

There are no problems, only tests to be passed.
