## **Wenukura**

December 4 was the birthday of a friend and entrepreneur: Pepe
Flores (known as Wenukura).

Pepe was the first person to put a website on the air in Chile, and the
founder of the first Chilean web development company. He has led famous
projects, from the first (and best) La Tercera newspaper site to the
Regata Web contest, although the project I like most is
[La Aurora de Chile Digita](https://www.auroradechile.cl/newtenberg/681/channel.html)l.
He currently runs Newtenberg.

On the site of [Levtraru](http://org.levtraru.com/655/channel.html), his
most important social-business venture, Pepe writes:

"We have set ourselves high goals, perhaps utopian ones, because we
believe that gives meaning to our existence. Building new companies
is a vehicle for generating well-being, and that is why we have chosen
it as our way of changing the world."\
Here is an example of what it means to be a knight errant in search of
the Holy Grail: what matters is that by making the journey the world
changes a little, with the hope that it's for the better (although that is impossible).

Congratulations, Pepe. I think you deserve more tributes than this
simple post, because without you, and several others like you, this blog
probably wouldn't exist, and neither would much of what's on the Chilean web.
