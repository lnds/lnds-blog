[Ricardo](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426081001/http://www.ricardodiaz.org/) leaves me a note about this blog's anniversary. [Carlos](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426081001/http://eldiabloenlosdetalles.net/) asks me what's going on, since I haven't written in a while.

My answer to both is that work has been very intense these last few weeks, and I'm also reorganizing how I use my time.

As they say, a blog is like a shark: it has to keep moving, or it dies.

But this blog won't die, least of all now that [Jens Hardings](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426081001/http://hardings.cl/) sends me this [news story](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426081001/http://hardings.cl/prensa/emol-ioi_chile-20060824.pdf) about Chile's participation in the [IOI 2006](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426081001/http://www.ioi2006.org/), which apparently has to be taken with a grain of salt because, as almost always, the journalists didn't do their job properly. But we'll look at that over the weekend (hint: check the official IOI site).

For now, though, let's celebrate, let's raise a glass to our first year, and thank you for continuing to read me.
