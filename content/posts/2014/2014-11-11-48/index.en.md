Today I turn forty-eight; the good news is that in hexadecimal I can say
it's only thirty. It doesn't really matter, it's just a geek joke, I
have no problem with my age.

This birthday in particular has been wonderful, because I'm just back
from an extraordinary vacation with my family in a magical place, but
the magic didn't come so much from the place as from the experience of
being kids again.

That is what makes you younger: knowing that in the end your family is
fine, seeing them content, seeing them happy.

I have said it several times: you can't be happy. Happiness is not
something permanent, it's something temporary, a state, to be enjoyed as
intensely as possible. Same as sadness. How can you enjoy the good
without knowing the bad? It is that fundamental duality of nature that
we must learn to appreciate. (Actually it's not a duality, it's a
multiplicity, but I'm not going to get any deeper today.)

It's a great day, a beautiful day. I'm proud of the life I have led,
with its storm clouds, its sunsets and its glorious dawns. Forty-eight
trips around the sun; I hope several more orbits are coming.

And as is tradition on this blog, I'm leaving you a video as a gift,
this time one of my favorites, \"Dreamer\", by Supertramp, performed by
Roger Hodgson.

It's important to be a dreamer, but you have to go beyond where the
character in this song stands and take action to make those dreams come
true.
