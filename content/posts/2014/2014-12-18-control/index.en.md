> "If everything seems under control, you\'re just not going fast
> enough."\
> -- Mario Andretti

Add controls, one after another, in a long chain, so that everything
comes out without errors, and all you will achieve is making things
slower.

Agile is flexibility plus speed. You can be fast and flexible and still
keep control; the trick is not to overdo the controls, to work
incrementally and to accept a degree of uncertainty. Accept that you
can't control everything, accept the possibility of failure.

Deliver gradually. You can move forward step by step: continuous
improvement.

[Invest your time in reducing latency](http://www.infoq.com/presentations/economy-team-slow-down).
Gradual change, a fixed percentage per year, in the way you do things
will bring you great benefits.

Compound interest is the most powerful force in the universe. If you
invest 30% a year in improving, you will get spectacular results in just
a few years.
