For several years now one of my "dreams" has been to publish books, not
one but several, which is pretty ambitious really :). In part, much of
what I have written on this blog has been preparation for that, and
now I feel ready to take that step.

For some time I have been exploring new media for spreading my ideas.
One of the first has been the regular chat we hold with my friends
Marco and Ubaldo on [El Test Acido](http://www.eltestacido.cl). There,
the conversation in front of the webcam, with several of you
joining in, has been an enriching and entertaining experience.

Another avenue has been taking this blog to the public through various
presentations, which I have given at some universities and, recently,
with my participation in StartechConf 2013 ([here is the video](https://vimeo.com/77928658)).

<http://www.amazon.com/Naturaleza-del-Software-Spanish-Edition-ebook/dp/B00GF0FV1C>

But collecting many of these writings in a book is
the most ambitious step I have taken. So, taking advantage of the
possibilities technology gives us, I have decided to self-publish, and
as of today my first e-book is available on Amazon:
[La Naturaleza del Software Volumen 1](http://www.amazon.com/Naturaleza-del-Software-Spanish-Edition-ebook/dp/B00GF0FV1C) (in Spanish).

Starting today you can read a selection taken from the first
seven years of this blog. There are 32 articles, specially edited and in
some cases expanded, grouped into 4 parts, in around 160
pages, which I hope you enjoy as much as I enjoyed selecting
the texts.

This first edition is available in digital format for the Kindle
e-reader. You don't need a Kindle device to read it;
there are apps for iOS, Android, Windows Phone, PC and Mac.
You will find all of these apps on this Amazon page:
<http://www.amazon.com/gp/feature.html?docId=1000493771>.

I am working on the paper version of the book, though I am
looking into publishing it on Amazon itself, or
through some other publisher (if anyone knows
one who might be interested, you can reach me in the comments). So
when I have the paper edition I will let you know.

I hope to continue this adventure with new volumes and formats
(on other platforms too, such as iBooks). There is a lot of
material that was left out and deserves to be collected in this special
way.

So that is what I wanted to share with you. I am happy to
be able to hand you this work of mine, which I hope you receive with the same
enthusiasm with which you have received the posts of the last eight years on
this site. I look forward to seeing you there, and I thank you in advance for your interest in this
book. It is like a new child you bring into the world. Now it is time for it
to fly and live on its own, and I, like any father, am
proud of it\...
