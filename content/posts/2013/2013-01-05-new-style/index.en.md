New year, new style and a change of publishing platform.

Starting today this blog will use [octopress](http:///www.octopress.org)
as its content generation platform.

Another novelty this year is that all the content is
available in [this github repository](https://github.com/lnds/lnds-content), under a Creative
Commons 3.0 license.

It's not just about having a backup; it is also a way to
preserve and spread the content of this blog. With all the "source
code" of my content from these years available, you can
help me improve it and spread it. If you have used github you know that if
you find a "bug" (an error) in my posts you can get the source,
fix it directly on github and let me know through a "pull
request". That's the magic of github, and we can use it to improve the
quality of the content.

The other thing this medium gives me is the flexibility to take
these articles to other formats, such as an e-book, for
example, a project I have been putting off for a long time and that
I can probably make happen this year.

Thanks again for continuing to read me. I'll be waiting for you, and may we all have
a very good 2013.
