In 2011 I put together [an anthology](/en/blog/lnds/2011/05/01/anthology/) of several
posts published in the first years of this blog. Now, with
my [ebook](http://t.co/xAz5Jftij7) just out (it contains a
selection/edit of 32 articles from the blog's first seven years),
I decided to write this list of what I consider the best articles of
the last three years, to complement that first anthology. This
anthology also includes posts written on
[Akarrú](http://www.akarru.com/), my other blog.

Since the previous anthology was done in May 2011, I am going to include
posts from the latter part of 2011. Here we go:

## **2011**

-   [A model of trust](/en/blog/lnds/2011/05/22/a-model-of-trust/)
    where I discuss the famous trust equation, which helps us
    guide the way we relate to others, in business, at
    work, in our own family.
-   [MIT's hacker culture](/en/blog/lnds/2011/06/15/mit-hacker-culture/)
    a selection of great hacks from that famous university.
-   [What is legitimate](/en/blog/lnds/2011/06/20/what-is-legitimate/)
    what is legitimate in each society and in its time?
-   [Protector of mankind](https://www.akarru.com/blog/2011/06/05/protector-de-la-humanidad/)
    The story of Thor and how he could go on protecting us\...
-   [A monkey's bite](https://www.akarru.com/blog/2011/07/31/la-mordida-de-un-mono/):
    The king who could have kept the peace does not survive a monkey
    bite, and the bloody dictator survives a bullet wound.
    Ironies of history.
-   [The paradox of common sense](/en/blog/lnds/2011/06/28/the-paradox-of-common-sense/)
    the first in my series on common sense, or the nonexistence of
    it.
-   [Irrational decisions](/en/blog/lnds/2011/07/05/irrational-decisions/)
    We are irrational: 90% of our decisions and actions are
    governed by our unconscious self\...
-   [Imagined causes](/en/blog/lnds/2011/10/22/imagined-causes/)
    the post hoc fallacy, or false causality\...
-   [The strategy paradox](/en/blog/lnds/2011/08/02/the-strategy-paradox/):
    Managing strategic uncertainty can be an interesting
    exercise that lets us handle various possible
    scenarios, and perhaps get ahead of some of the future's surprises.
-   [Framing](/en/blog/lnds/2011/08/24/framing/) We
    human beings are cognitive misers\...
-   [If it ain't broke, don't fix it](https://www.akarru.com/blog/2011/10/01/si-no-est-roto-no-lo-repares/)
    sometimes the intentions are good, the results are not\...
-   [Poetry, calligrams and the beauty of code](/en/blog/lnds/2011/09/11/poetry-calligrams-and-the-beauty-of-code/)
    can an algorithm, or the code that expresses it, be aesthetically
    beautiful?
-   [Salads and algorithms](/en/blog/lnds/2011/10/16/salads-and-algorithms/)
    how Map Reduce works, plus a salad recipe.
-   [Reading comprehension](/en/blog/lnds/2011/11/12/reading-comprehension/)
    The waterfall development method was never proposed by anyone,
    seriously.
