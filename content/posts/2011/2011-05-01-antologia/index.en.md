# Anthology 

Over almost 6 years I have written close to 1,000 articles on this
blog. Since new readers come along every so often, I decided to
publish this anthology of the posts I like best. I took the chance to
revisit them and make a few corrections. They are sorted by year of
publication, with a cap of 6 per year :confused:, I hope you enjoy them.

## **2005**

-   [The legend of the space pen](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/04/the-legend-of-the-space-pen/),  sometimes the most effective solutions are the simplest ones.

-   [The legs of salamanders](/en/blog/lnds/2005/11/21/salamander-legs/), from Alzheimer's to the future of genetic engineering.

## **2006**
