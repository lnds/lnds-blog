Language is the most precious and at the same time the most dangerous
good that has been given to man. -- Hölderlin

Man is a god when he dreams, a beggar when he reflects. -- Hölderlin

My mission is to kill time, and time's is to kill me in turn. One feels
at ease among murderers -- Cioran

Skepticism is the intoxication of the dead end. -- Cioran

Golden rule: leave an incomplete image of yourself. -- Cioran

Anything I can reach with a ladder does not interest me. -- Wittgenstein

In my thinking, as in any man's, hang the withered remains of my
earlier (already dead) thoughts. -- Wittgenstein

Nothing is so difficult as not deceiving oneself. -- Wittgenstein

To be conscious is not to be in time -- T.S. Eliot

Only through time time is conquered -- T.S. Eliot

Nothing is more fertile than ignorance that is aware of
itself. -- Ortega y Gasset

Whenever you teach, teach people at the same time to doubt what you
teach. -- Ortega y Gasset

Individually or collectively, youth needs to believe itself, a priori,
superior. Of course it is wrong, but that is precisely the great right
of youth: it has the right to be wrong with impunity. --
Ortega y Gasset

Only he who says nothing is incapable of contradicting himself -- Unamuno

The way to hit the nail on the head once is to hit the horseshoe a
hundred times. -- Unamuno
