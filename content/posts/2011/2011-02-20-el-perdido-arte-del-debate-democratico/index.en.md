It seems to me that one of our problems as a country is that we have
lost the ability to debate. Debate seems to have been reduced to
gathering as many Twitter followers as possible in order to impose an
idea, or, if that space is not enough, people set up their own social
network where those who think alike publish long essays proving how
wrong everyone else is.
Nowhere is this more obvious than in the energy debate. It worries me 
([and I'm not the only one](http://ideaschile.wordpress.com/2011/02/19/hidroaysen-vs-patagonia-sin-represa/))
that decisions are being made (or not made) based on perceptions,
without any rational basis or grounding, without debating what the
consequences and the best alternatives are.

We have lost the ability to debate democratically. That is why I want
to point you to this TED video, so we start keeping in mind that it is
essential to raise the level of our debates, because we could be
mortgaging the future of our country.
