In this video Emiliano Salinas reflects on the 4 levels of
civil response to violence. The step a citizenry takes when,
instead of seeing itself as a victim of its situation, it becomes
responsible and engaged, and takes the future of its country into its own hands.

The first level is denial and apathy, the state of not acknowledging
that there are problems. The citizenry has to be moved to the second level, which
is recognition, and out of it fear arises in many people.
Fear is better, because it moves citizens to do something. But
fear can shut people up in their homes. So it is necessary
to shake people up and take them to the third level, action. Now, for
many people acting requires courage, rage. "I can't take this
situation anymore, let's do something!", and then violence will emerge. Faced with
violence, what has to be done is to move to the fourth level, that of
non-violent, coordinated, peaceful action, which does not mean passivity; it requires
determination.

There are statements in the video that are plainly chauvinistic, and by
the end it feels like we are listening to a pep rally speech, but I think
identifying the 4 levels of action is a fairly
reasonable idea, and it is the part of the video worth keeping.
