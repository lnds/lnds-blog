> "The role of progressive government is to maximize our freedom—and
> protection and empowerment do just that. Protection is there to
> guarantee freedom from harm, from want, from fear. Empowerment is
> there to maximize freedom to achieve your goals."
>
> -- George Lakoff, [The Political Mind](http://t.co/q3siTc2), pages
> 47 and 48.

Notes:

(*) SEC, which Lakoff mentions in this passage as part of government empowerment: **Securities and Exchange Commission**, the
US agency whose mission is to enforce the laws
that regulate the securities industry in the United States. It is like our
Superintendencia de Valores y Seguros (SVS) in Chile
