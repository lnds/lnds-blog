> Emilio Lledó, professor of the History of Philosophy and member of the
> Royal Spanish Academy, also champions mathematics as a light to shine on
> a world of manipulated information. \"This science is a constant struggle
> with the truth because in it, in its exactness, there is no room for
> lying ideas\". Lledó recalls its etymology: from the Greek *máthema*,
> to learn. And not only to learn, but to experience. And not only to
> experience, but to deduce. And not only to deduce, but to prove. And not
> only to prove, but to be in contact with what is true. \"And all
> this\", he laments, \"cannot be very fashionable in a universe that
> tends toward falsehood\".
