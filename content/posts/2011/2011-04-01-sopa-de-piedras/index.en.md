Three soldiers were on their way home from a long war. After several days
on the road, the sight of a village gladdened their hearts: they hoped to
taste a hot meal at last.

The townspeople shut themselves up in their houses; war and looting had
taught them to hide what little they had.\
All the soldiers got was a big pot and some water.

They set up in the middle of the village, lit a fire and dropped three
stones into the pot.

This stirred the villagers' curiosity. \"What are you doing?\" one of them
asked. \"Stone soup, but the truth is it gets better with a bit of
carrot\". A woman ran home and brought some carrots, and the grateful
soldiers tipped them into the soup. \"With onions and potatoes it's even
better\", somebody remarked. And so others kept bringing more ingredients,
along with new comments and suggestions for improving the flavor of the
soup, which encouraged someone else to bring an ingredient.

When it was good and hearty and tasty, the soldiers took the stones out of
the soup, served themselves and shared out the best meal the villagers had
tasted after many months of hardship.

Don't know how to motivate people? Think it's time to change things, to
break the apathy? Make stone soup
