Not that anyone has asked me the question, but I bring it up because of
this post by [Fred Wilson](http://www.avc.com/a_vc/about.html): 
[Do You Ever Get Bored Of Blogging?](http://www.avc.com/a_vc/2011/02/do-you-ever-get-bored-of-blogging.html):

> "The truth is I never get bored of writing. It is something that came
> relatively late in life for me\.... It's a hobby, something I do to
> entertain and educate myself and I enjoy it very much. I love putting
> the puzzle that are my thoughts together every day."

But it turns out there is a side effect:

> "But an unintended consequence of this writing hobby is that I've
> developed an audience and a public persona. I didn't set out to do
> that. But it happened. And now I've got a responsibility to serve the
> audience and manage the public persona. At least I feel that
> responsibility."

I believe, and Fred Wilson makes it explicit, that this is where the
danger lies, and the reason so many people give up on writing blogs.
You can end up feeling trapped, obligated, and that produces stress and
a reaction of boredom. The consequence is an abandoned blog. Which then
forces you, every so often, to show some sign that you are still alive,
especially if your blog is very popular and you have not written in a
while.

I hope I never have to feel obligated to write for others, for you, my
300 subscribers. I like sharing what I write and think with you,
sharing [this box of chocolates](/en/blog/lnds/2009/07/30/four-years/) that
is my blog, now in its sixth year.

It is possible, though rather unlikely, that I will leave a couple of
old articles scheduled for republication.

So this blog is officially declared on summer vacation. Thank you very
much for your understanding ;)
