Time is more valuable than gold.

While gold can fluctuate, time is always scarce.

Don't waste time, you can't get it back. Maybe you have no money today, but
perhaps tomorrow you will. This moment, though: can you get it back?

Time is the most valuable natural resource. Let's not waste it.
