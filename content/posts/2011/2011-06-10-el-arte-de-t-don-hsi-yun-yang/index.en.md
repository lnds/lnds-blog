Try this:

    curl http://www.de.ioccc.org/2000/dhyang.c > dhyang.c

    cat dhyang.c

    cc dhyang.c -o saitou 

    ./saitou > aku.c

    cat aku.c 

    cc aku.c -o soku 

    ./soku > soku.c 

    cat soku.c 

    cc soku.c -o zan 

    ./zan > zan.c 

    cat zan.c 

    cc zan.c -o aku 

    ./aku | diff - aku.c

There is more art of this kind at <http://uguu.org/omoikane.html>
