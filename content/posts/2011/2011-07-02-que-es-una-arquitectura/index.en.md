Last Thursday, June 30, at the Colegio de Ingenieros de Chile (Chile's
professional association of engineers), my friend Juan Carlos Barroux
[\@jbarroux](https://twitter.com/#!/jbarroux) gave the talk "What is an
architecture?".\
The talk was organized by the
[DCC8090](http://www.meetup.com/dcc8090/) group.

We took the chance to record the talk for [El Test
Acido](http://www.eltestacido.cl/), and Juan Carlos has published his
talk under a Creative Commons license. Here are the video and the
slides. If you're interested in future talks, I suggest following
\@dcc8090 on Twitter, and our group on Meetup: <http://www.meetup.com/dcc8090/>
