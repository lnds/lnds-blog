There have been 3 important battles on the Nile. The first in 47 BC,
when Julius Caesar backed Cleopatra against her Ptolemy siblings. The
second in Napoleon's time, between the fleets of England and France in
Aboukir Bay. And yesterday's battle, between the Egyptian people and
the forces of the dictator Mubarak:
