2011 was a hard year. But personally I had some satisfactions, and
achievements that let me come out with a positive balance. A year being
hard doesn't make it a bad year.

We live in a time when people don't reflect much, so the clarification
matters. The trouble with the digital age is that it has led people to
think in binary, 0 or 1. The year is good or bad. We computer people are
partly to blame for that: on facebook, people are friends or not, like
or not, +1 or nothing. I invite you to see life as it really is, a
continuous reality, not discrete, not digital. With highs and lows, with
states, with variables, with contradictions and few certainties.

It's time to end the year and start the new one, which we'll talk about
soon enough. It's time to reboot. But this post is about closing out,
about taking stock. I leave you with my personal selection of the
articles I think came out best this year, and I'll see you in the next
one.
