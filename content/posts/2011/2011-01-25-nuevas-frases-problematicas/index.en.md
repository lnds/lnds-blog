A new, extended edition of [problematic sayings](/en/blog/lnds/2006/10/12/problematic-sayings/):

> Every problem has a solution; if not, it isn't a problem\...

In life there are no problems, only solutions we haven't
found yet.

In practice, a problem is only what intelligence can
solve. **Hermann Keyerling**

Emergency Principle: if you keep calm when everyone else is losing their
head, you clearly haven't grasped the problem.

I know of no better motivation than problems.

Facing it, always facing it, is the way to solve the problem.
Face it!**. \*Joseph** **Conrad\***

> The problem doesn't matter, the solution does.
>
> The solution is in the problem.

The formulation of a problem is more important than its solution.
**Albert Einstein.***

If the problem has a solution, worrying is no use, and if the
problem has no solution, worrying is no use.

Better to stop worrying and start doing.

Most people spend more time talking about problems than
facing them. **Henry Ford**


> In this life you have to be a solution, not a problem.
>
> We build our problems ourselves.

If you have a problem and you can solve it, don't worry,
solve it.\
If you have a problem and you can't solve it, don't worry either,
forget it.

Problems only get solved by having something to think about.

Problems are messages\... **Shakti Gawain**

> One thing is clear: there are more problemologists than solutionologists.

If you're not part of the solution, you're part of the problem

If you have problems, remember it could always be worse.

Not solving problems guarantees a bigger problem. **Joaquín
Almunia**

That's the typical nonsense from someone who has never had a serious problem.

People don't understand that problems are, and will be, the reason to live
tomorrow.

Don't talk about your problems unless it's to find a solution for them.

If a problem has a solution, why worry? If it doesn't,
why worry?

There are no problems, only tests to be passed.

Don't come to me with problems, bring me solutions!

Better to be part of the solution than part of the problem.

There is only one way to be happy, and that is to stop worrying about what
is beyond our control. **Epictetus of Phrygia**
