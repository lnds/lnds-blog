Via [the Malaprensa blog](http://personales.ya.com/josumezo/malaprensa/2009/01/el-obammetro.html) I found out about [this site](http://www.politifact.com/truth-o-meter/promises/), where they will be tracking a compilation of 500 promises made by Barack Obama, which will be rated as kept, compromise, broken, stalled, in the works and no action.

It would be good to set up something similar in our country, when the next president takes office, don't you think?
