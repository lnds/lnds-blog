[Via Boing Boing](http://www.boingboing.net/2009/07/01/richard-dawkins-help.html),
I learn that [Richard Dawkins](http://richarddawkins.net/) is funding a
summer youth camp for kids aged 8 to 17, with the idea of
teaching them critical thinking.

Along with the traditional activities of canoeing and swimming, the kids
who take part in the five-day camp in Somerset will learn
about rational skepticism, moral philosophy, ethics and evolution. They
will also be taught how to debunk phenomena such as crop circles and
telepathy. The [post explains that](http://www.boingboing.net/2009/07/01/richard-dawkins-help.html):

> "In the Invisible Unicorn Challenge, any child who can
> prove that unicorns do not exist will win a £10 note - which
> features an image of Charles Darwin, the father of evolutionary
> theory - signed by Dawkins, Britain's most prominent
> atheist."

That last announcement made me very sad\....
