> "To read what is good you must not read what is bad, because life is short and time and energy are limited."
>
> "That is why it is so important to know the art of not reading. It consists in not reading whatever happens to occupy the general public at the moment, such as political and ecclesiastical pamphlets, novels, poetry and so on, some of which go through several editions."
>
> -- Arthur Schopenhauer
