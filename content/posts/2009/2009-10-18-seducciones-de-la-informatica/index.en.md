Ricardo Galli (the creator of [Meneame](http://www.meneame.net/)) recently gave a very interesting talk called [the seductions of computing](http://www.vivamerica.com/madrid/programacion/innovacion/atomos-versus-bits/ricardo-galli-las-seducciones-de-la-informatica#top)". I recommend watching the video, it's quite good, and then reading [this companion note](http://gallir.wordpress.com/2009/10/13/anotaciones-cientificas-de-seducciones-de-la-informatica/) on his blog.

For the young people wondering what the value of studying computing is, I hope this video inspires you.
