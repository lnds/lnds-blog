It seems fitting right now to recall one of the "more interesting" races of characters from the Star Trek series. I mean the [Ferengi](http://memory-alpha.org/es/wiki/Ferengi), a bunch of greedy, cowardly hypercapitalists, famous for their "[Rules of Acquisition](http://memory-alpha.org/es/wiki/Reglas_de_adquisici%C3%B3n)".

It's good to know what the Rules of Acquisition say, because I have a suspicion that the Ferengi have established a colony on our planet, and they seem to operate behind the fronts of pharmacy chains, supermarkets and stock exchanges.

I've seen a few of them around (pay special attention at business seminars) ....
