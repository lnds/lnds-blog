## **Science and art**

> Our discussion indicates that computer programming is by now both a
> science and an art, and that the two aspects nicely complement each
> other. Apparently most authors who examine such a question come to
> this same conclusion, that their subject is both a science and an art,
> whatever their subject is \[...\]

Excerpt from Donald Knuth's 1974 Turing Award lecture, *Computer
Programming as an Art*.
