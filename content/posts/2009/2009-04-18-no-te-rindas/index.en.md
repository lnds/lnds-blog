This week [my queen, my wife, my love](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ilknNpmqNoI) had her birthday.

The other day, while we were in the car, Peter Gabriel's [Don't give up](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDJ1qzuptd8) came on. I told her about the video, and about part of the lyrics, and about how I felt she was my support in the same sense the song describes.


In hard times, having your partner and your kids at your side can be the great support that helps you get through.


These are hard times for a lot of people. I'm coming out of the eclipse, but many are still in it. To them, and to their partners, their supports, I dedicate this song.
