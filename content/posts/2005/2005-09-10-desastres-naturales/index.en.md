There are no natural disasters. The effects of Katrina or of other natural
phenomena become disasters when they affect humanity. To
nature, our constructions are insignificant.

Disasters of this kind are cultural, or they are the foreseeable result
of [the consequences of political
decisions](http://laotraorilla.blog-city.com/el_verdadero_rostro_de_la_tragedia.htm).

Unfortunately, those who make the decisions follow their own agendas
and don't consider the consequences of the [climate
change](http://www.globalwarming.org/) we are suffering. Despite
the [Kyoto
agreement](http://unfccc.int/essential_background/kyoto_protocol/items/2830.php),
greenhouse gas emissions [have increased in
Europe](http://reports.eea.eu.int/technical_report_2005_4/en). The
New Orleans tragedy is not the first, nor will it be the last. The
problem is that things will get worse.
