The image shows how the perception that google is evil went up right
when Google launched its
[IPO](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPO).

[![](https://www.realmeme.com/Main/evilindex/corporations/googleDejanews.png)](http://www.realmeme.com/Main/evilindex/index.jsp)

I find the analysis of [memes](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme) used
at [Realmeme](http://www.realmeme.com/Main/miner.jsp) interesting. A
few years ago I 
[wrote some programs](http://groups.google.com/group/google.public.web-apis/search?group=google.public.web-apis&q=eduardo+diaz&qt_g=1)
that used the [Google API](http://www.google.com/apis/) to calculate
popularity between memes, but it hadn't occurred to me to plot it over
time. I'll see if it can be done, and in whatever time I have left I
promise to spruce up the program and make it available to anyone
interested.
