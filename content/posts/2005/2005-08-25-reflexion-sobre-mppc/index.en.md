Fernando Flores has spoken out about the MPPC campaign.

On that, I have to say the ugly part isn't which operating system the
PC in question comes with, or even the hardware. That's irrelevant. The
ugly part is that the sponsorship goes to the same people as always and
not to the small business owners, who sell better quality PCs at lower
prices.

(There used to be a comment here about the sponsorship. Thanks to Paolo
Colonello for clarifying that the government only gave its endorsement,
not sponsorship, so I apologize for the comment)

see [atina chile](http://www.atinachile.cl/node/3436)

Thanks, Paolo, for the clarification.\
Let's call in the little ducks (the state bank's mascots) to the
rescue, for example

Why not give loans at Banco del Estado so people can buy their first
PC, at rates that would be far lower than the ones the big department
stores will charge? With the freedom to buy from anyone, in particular
from the thousands of business owners who sell hardware up and down our
country, who could benefit much more from a campaign like this one.

But it seems that what matters is for the big stores to keep capturing
more cardholders. Now that really does our country good.

It seems that what matters is improving the digital literacy figures.
Making it look like the government is doing something, never mind if
it's done badly.

By the way, for a while now there have been something like 7 million
users with internet access, and computers with extraordinary capacity,
in their pockets and purses, at costs far below US\$ 100. Those
computers are called cell phones ;) and for a while now we've been able
to do fun things with them besides making calls\...
