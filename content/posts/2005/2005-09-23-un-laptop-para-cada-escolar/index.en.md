Here is the email my friend and business partner [Marco Antonio Zúñiga](http://www.maz.cl/) is circulating

> Let's build a different Chile NOW ....
> 
> Argentina is setting us an example, even without having the resources (see the end of this email for the Argentine case).
> 
> In Chile it is only a matter of will.
> 
> IN CHILE IT IS ONLY A MATTER OF WILL.
> 
> "Campaign:"  
A LAPTOP FOR EVERY CHILEAN CHILD IN SUBSIDIZED OR MUNICIPAL SCHOOLS.
> 
> Background:
> 
> Any Chilean can look up, on the Ministry of Education's site, the value of the subsidies for subsidized or municipal schools.
> 
> [http://www.comunidadescolar.cl/ComunidadEscolar/documentacion/Subvencion/valor_subvenciones_FEBRERO_2005.pdf](http://www.comunidadescolar.cl/ComunidadEscolar/documentacion/Subvencion/valor_subvenciones_FEBRERO_2005.pdf)
> 
> The average monthly school subsidy is approximately CLP 22,000 to 23,000 in primary education and CLP 27,000 in secondary education.
> 
> US$ 100 is approximately 2.5 "months" of the subsidy.
> 
> Proposal:
> 
> Let's raise the annual subsidy by "JUST" 22% (a little more than two months), JUST ONCE ... And give a computer to every schoolchild.
> 
> We don't quite know what to do with the copper surplus today?  
Let's take the chance to invest in our children and teenagers.
> 
> Let's stop holding 634,275 meetings to talk about the subject, and writing and reading 10,435 studies that say the problem lies in "Education" and the "Digital Divide".
> 
> Let's do something NOW. We have the resources and we can. It is only a problem of will.
> 
> It can be done ... It can be done ... At the very least, let's get our country to consider the possibility.  
Let's at least ask ourselves the question once.
> 
> Let's dream the future.
> 
> A miserable US$ 100 per child, once, can make the difference for our future as a country and for our children.
> 
> Regards to all ...
> 
> Marco Antonio Zuniga
