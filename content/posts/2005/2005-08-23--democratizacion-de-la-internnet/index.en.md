A long time ago, before the earth had cooled, we could set up our own
servers, plug them into the network, and there we were, publishing our
content on the internet. It could be a gopher, an ftp, a BBS, or a
newsgroup.

Berners-Lee gave us the web as a gift, and the innovators ruined
everything. The bubble burst and we all got soaked :)

A quote from Flores's blog:

> Moore is also the author of the book The Death of Competition, which
> deals with business strategies, and he points out that blogs and RSS
> make "the democratization of services on the internet" a reality

What!?

Back when there were no ISPs and the internet wasn't a business, and we
had\
total control of our ports, that's when the internet really was
democratic. Read Smart Mobs, which explains better how we went from a
scheme of total freedom to the tyranny of the ISPs.

So in reality blogs, and better yet wikis, are winning back ground that
was lost thanks to the innovators of the internet.

I'm not against entrepreneurship, or against new businesses based on
blogs. I don't quite see where the money is, Show me the money, but
when there is money involved, it's hard to have more democracy.
Heisenberg's principle:

Delta(Democracy) x Delta(Capitalism) = Constant.
