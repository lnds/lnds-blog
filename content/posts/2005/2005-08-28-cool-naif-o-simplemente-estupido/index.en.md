I don't know if publishing your exact GPS location is cool. I, for one,
think it's risky.

For example, the
site [https://wiki.ubuntu.com/UbuntuWorldWide](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180549/https://wiki.ubuntu.com/UbuntuWorldWide)
has a list of users with their physical address.

I'm not going to get into the paranoia of publishing my physical
address and the fear of a visit from a burglar or a serial killer :).

The problem is that a hacker can do [social engineering](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180549/http://www.securityfocus.com/infocus/1527)
with that information. If you care about security, you shouldn't
publish these things, no matter how cool it is.
