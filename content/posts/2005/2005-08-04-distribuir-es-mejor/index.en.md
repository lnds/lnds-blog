"Distribuir" is better

In my previous post,
[Sindicar](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/04/to-syndicate-in-spanish/),
I argued that this word does not exist in Spanish as a translation of
"syndicate", and that as a last resort we could use the term
*Sindicación Web*.

[Drupal](http://www.drupal.org/) gave me the answer: the right word is
*DISTRIBUIR* (to distribute). No need to invent neologisms or accept
anglicisms.
