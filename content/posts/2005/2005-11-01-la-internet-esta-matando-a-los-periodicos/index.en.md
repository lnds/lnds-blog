Media Daily News [reports](https://web.archive.org/web/20080419063530/http://publications.mediapost.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=Articles.showArticleHomePage&art_aid=35690)
that 2005 will go down as one of the worst years for newspapers in
history, and 2006 doesn't look promising. Online content keeps
generating more readers and more ad money, although it only accounts
for 5% of newspapers' total revenue.
