This is a new project we've started at [AmericaEscribe](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180446/http://www.americaescribe.org/). For those who don't know, [AmericaEscribe](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180446/http://www.americaescribe.org/) is an aggregator of Latin American blogs. For now, that project's sources are hand-picked at the discretion of a group of volunteer editors.

However, to make things more democratic, and because a good social bookmarking site in Spanish is badly needed, we've created [BlogMemes.com](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180446/http://www.blogmemes.com/), a clone of the famous [Digg.com](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180446/http://www.digg.com/), but aimed at a more general scope.

We invite you to contribute the stories that interest you. To avoid spam, the site requires you to register first.

If you're a PHP or JavaScript programmer and you're interested in taking part in the project, get in touch at admin@americaescribe.org, because you can help us.

A meme is a unit of cultural transmission, according to Dawkins, who wrote about this in his famous book The Selfish Gene. To learn more about memes, see the [Wikipedia article](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180446/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme).
