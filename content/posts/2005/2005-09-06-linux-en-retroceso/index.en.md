[This article](http://web.archive.org/web/20090426080906/http://www.silicon.com/research/specialreports/opensource/0,3800004943,39151835,00.htm)
on silicon tells how Unilever is backing out of its project to
migrate everything to Linux. According to Unilever's CIO: "the cost benefits
originally used to justify the risk of the migration are no longer
there."

The idea was to migrate its Unix servers to Linux on Itanium, and then
migrate SAP to run on Linux.

Unilever has an 800 million euro infrastructure, and the
project projected savings of 63 million. In any case they will make
changes, but on a much smaller scale.
