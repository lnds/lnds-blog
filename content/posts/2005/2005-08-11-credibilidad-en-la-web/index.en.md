Speaking of the [case of the pregnant man](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/08/the-pregnant-man-and-the-transgenic-mouse/),
over at Cazuela de Pinguino, Carlos writes about the reaction of the [Diario
Austral de
Temuco](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180549/http://www.australtemuco.cl/prontus4_noticias/antialone.html?page=http://www.australtemuco.cl/prontus4_noticias/site/artic/20050809/pags/20050809021545.html).

The phenomenon is interesting enough that there are researchers working
on the subject:\
[Stanford Web Credibility
Project](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180549/http://credibility.stanford.edu/).

There is an interesting paper, published at SIGCHI, [The Elements of
Computer
Credibility](http://replay.waybackmachine.org/20060211180549/http://portal.acm.org/citation.cfm?doid=302979.303001).
You have to be an ACM member to download it, but those of us interested
in the subject can get in touch by email.
