I don't know how much Senator Fernando Flores paid to have his
[site](http://www.fernandoflores.cl/blog/) built, but it doesn't sit
right with me that [Blue Company](http://www.bluecompany.biz/) puts in
the footer: *Site developed by blue company*, when it is obvious they
grabbed [Drupal](http://www.drupal.org/) and installed it.

I give them credit for the graphic design, but at least give Drupal its
credit. I think the right thing would be to put "site developed by Blue
Company, supporting software Drupal", or something along the lines of
"empowered by drupal".

Because don't go thinking that Blue Company develops CMS technologies.
If we are going to use open source, let's follow the open source ethic.
