These things are so obvious, yet nobody talks about them: google has had
a currency converter for quite some time.

If you want to know how many Chilean pesos 10 dollars are, search google
for

> 10 USD in CLP

And you will have the answer, or you can type the url directly:

<http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&lr=&q=10+USD+in+CLP&btnG=Search>

the important part is the value q=10+USD+in+CLP.
