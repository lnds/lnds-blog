I turn 39 today. It's been a good year overall, with its ups and downs, but with [some rewards too](http://www.eduardodiaz.org/2005/08/premio_innovacion_tecnologica.html).

I'm going to publish a post to be read 1 year from now. That's the neat thing about some blogs: they let you set the future date on which the post will be published. We'll read it when I'm 40.

There must be some 17,660,633 people having a birthday along with me!!

It's fun to know that [Roberto Matta](http://www.matta-art.com/), [Lucky Luciano](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_Luciano), [George Patton](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Patton), [Dostoevsky](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fi%C3%B3dor_Dostoievski), [Carlos Fuentes](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Fuentes) and [many more](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_11#Births) were also born on this day.
