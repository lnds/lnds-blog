I had already [told you ](/en/blog/lnds/2005/11/04/ulpn-2/) that on **November** 16 the ULPN project would be launched at [WSIS ](http://www.itu.int/wsis/tunis/)(World Summit on the Information Society) in Tunis. (Where [internet governance was also discussed](http://cdp.blogsome.com/2005/11/16/la-batalla-que-no-fue/)). In the Chilean delegation of the [magnificent seven](http://www.itu.int/wsis/participation/prepcom3/org_detail.asp?ID=916) there was no official from the Ministry of Education, though there was one from [Subtel ](http://www.itu.int/wsis/participation/prepcom3/del_detail.asp?ID=96027#rep)(how interesting that we can follow the movements of our government officials). The rest are staff from the permanent mission in Geneva.

## About the launch

Today I was thinking that you could make a lot of jokes playing on the surname Negroponte and Kofi Annan, but this is serious, so let's leave it to the reader's imagination.

[Shezzo ](http://nerdpride.org/blog/?p=26)and [Carlos](http://cdp.blogsome.com/2005/11/17/video-laptop-de-us100/) have reminded me of the subject, but I haven't posted because the truth is I still haven't been able to watch the video, and besides I've been on the [move](/en/blog/lnds/2005/11/18/mobility/), and the little they showed on TV yesterday was somewhat disappointing. It looked like a bunch of [mockups](http://www.fayerwayer.com/archivo/2005/11/lanzan_prototip.php), nothing working, although there's a photo out there of someone with a standard keyboard plugged into the machine. So I suspect that what Negroponte showed was the mockup, and that the prototype must be wired up and partially working, with outside help. That wouldn't be strange: when the Newton was launched there was a cable running from the Newton to a Mac, and the Newton was only acting as an input/output device.

So when I finally get access to the information, on cheaper and more decent bandwidth, I'll write up my impressions.

What matters is that several people [are blogging about it](http://blog.monkymotion.com/?p=70), and they realize what a fool our government has made of itself. But hey, another government is on its way, and let's hope things change for the better (I'm feeling optimistic today).
