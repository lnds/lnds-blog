Two stories of alleged abuse by google:

[The first](http://news.com.com/5208-12-0.html?forumID=1&threadID=8786&messageID=62962&start=-196)
is about a person who put up his small email forwarding site, and
google refused to include it in searches because his site supposedly
promoted pornography, nudism. He made his case, but now google refuses
to fix the problem.

The second story affects one of the most entertaining sites I've
visited:
[realmeme](http://news.com.com/5208-12-0.html?forumID=1&threadID=8786&start=0).
Remember the post I wrote about [whether google is evil or
good](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/23/google-good-or-evil/)? It was
based on realmeme and its evil index :smile:.

According to the owner of this site, google has cut his traffic coming
through the search engine. [Here is his
case](http://news.com.com/5208-12-0.html?forumID=1&threadID=8786&start=0).

As a bit of trivia, the title of this post was deliberately chosen so
that it reads google_es_malo.html ("google is evil"), to make up for
the previous post, which came out as google_es_bueno ("google is
good"). That way we keep the balance, and we make sure we don't get
censored\...\...
