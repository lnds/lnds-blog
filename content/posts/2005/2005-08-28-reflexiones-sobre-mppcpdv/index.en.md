One of the characteristics of adhocracy is that it is reactive, not
proactive. I'm not saying that's good or bad, it just is. It is born as
a reaction to something the community feels is out of line.

The way adhocracy shows itself is that groups of affected people get
activated, speak out, and when the goal is achieved, the movement
disappears.

Adhocracy exists because democracy has been discredited. People don't
trust the authorities.

Adhocracy cuts across the board. Those who commit to the cause in
question may hold totally opposite ideologies, or none at all. The only
thing uniting them is the sense that there is a problem that affects
them, or bothers them, and has to be solved.

In the case of MPPCPDV, those of us who support this movement think
there is an alternative to the government's proposal.\
So far MPPCDV has been mainly a media movement.
