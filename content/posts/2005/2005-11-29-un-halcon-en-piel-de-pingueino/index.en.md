Lockheed Martin [selected ](http://home.businesswire.com/portal/site/google/index.jsp?ndmViewId=news_view&newsId=20051128005133&newsLang=en)[RedHawk Linux](http://www.ccur.com/isd_solutions_redhawklinux.asp?o9=1) from the company [Concurrent ](http://www.ccur.com/)for the THAAD tactical defense missile program. Concurrent's real-time system will support the mission-critical testing of the defensive missiles.

Well, here's one way to make money with open source: sell to the defense industry.

I don't like it, but as far as I know the GPL doesn't prevent this use.
