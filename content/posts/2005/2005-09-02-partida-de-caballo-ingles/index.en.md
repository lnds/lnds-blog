Is MPPCDV running out of air on us?\
Could it be behaving like the [10Mega plan from VTR](/en/blog/lnds/2005/09/02/bandwidth/)?\
I look at the [number](http://mppc.tardis.cl/) and it seems stalled.\
[Flores offers to talk to the
president](http://www.fernandoflores.cl/node/644).

Beyond publishing banners and logos, and writing, I don't think there
is much more on offer.

In fact Jens Hardings put his finger on the main problem with MPPCDV:
there is no concrete alternative inside the community, no matter how
much altruism we put in. More details in [this
post](http://listas.inf.utfsm.cl/pipermail/linux/2005-August/021942.html).

I proposed that the solution be special loans to buy whatever PC you
want, with the configuration you care about and the operating system
you want, but buying from the small and medium-sized businesses that
are in this line of work. Couldn't Banco Estado, or CORFO, the state
development agency, make this possible?

That would level things out, because small businesses don't have the
financial muscle to offer credit the way the big department stores do.\
I believe the positive effect this would have on our economy would be
much greater than the big stores clearing out their inventory.

It's a concrete idea. Maybe it can't be done, but we need more things
along those lines. Hanging posters or wearing T-shirts amounts to
nothing more than snobbery with little effect.
