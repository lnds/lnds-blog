I received a comment from one of the mothers of the Cuban team that took part in the IOI.

For those who didn't read it, the post is called: [Cuba, the best in computing](/en/blog/lnds/2005/09/02/cuba-the-best-at-computing/)

The [IOI 2006](http://www.ioi2006.org/) will be held in Mérida, Yucatán, Mexico. According to the rules Chile could not compete, but it could join as an observer.

In other words, we have 2 years to prepare our computing olympic team. We're cooking something up along these lines, so I hope to have good news for you.
