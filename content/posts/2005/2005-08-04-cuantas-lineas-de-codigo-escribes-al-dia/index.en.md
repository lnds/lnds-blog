I think the reason many software development projects fail is the
separation between architects, analysts and programmers.

I believe this division causes communication problems; the distance
between the end user and the programmer is too great.

Agile methodologies promise to improve this. I think the best way is to
have a flat development hierarchy. Brooks's old Chief Programmer Team.
