After [finding, with Google Earth, the remains of an
ancient Roman villa near Parma in
Italy](http://www.nature.com/news/2005/050912/full/050912-6.html), the
enthusiastic [Luca Mori](http://www.quellidellabassa.org/) has founded
cyberarchaeology, putting up his site:
[cyberarchaeologist.net](http://cyberarchaeologist.net/)
