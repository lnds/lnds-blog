On Thursday, September 6, I gave a talk at the Universidad
Tecnológica Metropolitana to the students of the Civil Engineering
in Computer Science program. The talk is called ["Creators of
Universes"](http://www.slideshare.net/EduardoDiazCortes/creadores-de-universos).

The talk was motivational in nature, and reflects several of the ideas
laid out in earlier posts, in particular
[these](/en/blog/lnds/2012/05/09/what-is-programming/)
[three](/en/blog/lnds/2012/05/18/learning-to-program/)
[articles](/en/blog/lnds/2012/05/18/what-should-we-teach-new-developers/).
One of the things I liked most about the content of the talk is that
I talk about my team, those wonderful people who work with me at
my job, of whom I am proud, and about the way they
contribute to fulfilling our vision of service.

My team's work has a positive impact on the lives of millions of
Chileans. What we do doesn't get much public exposure, there are no
glamorous websites for you to visit, and you won't hear
much about our processes on television or in the media either. At most,
when some minister (of any government) announces a new benefit
for the population, you could presume that we will probably be
working to make it happen. That's why I am
proud of my team, because they are very aware of how
important it is that they do things well, and they do their best
to that end. That's why I am grateful to them, and that's why I
included them and talked a lot about them in that talk.

The other thing I liked about this talk was the reception from the young people.
They were very kind, and I noticed a lot of interest. They laughed quite a bit, and for
a guy as dull as I am that means a lot; I think I got their attention.
I want to thank Sebastián Salazar, who invited me, and the kids from the
student union, as well as Mauro Castillo, Director of the School of
Computer Science, and Sara Rojas, faculty members who were present (I hope
I didn't step on too many toes :wink: ).
