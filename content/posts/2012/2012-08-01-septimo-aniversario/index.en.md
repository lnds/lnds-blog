After several false starts, on August 1, 2005 I published the
[first post](/blog/2005/08/no-puedes-proteger-el-software.html)
on this blog.

Today this space turns seven!

So we will celebrate these seven years with the participation of several
friends who have agreed to contribute some articles, which I will be
publishing over the next few days. To all of them, my thanks in advance for
being willing.

We will also revisit some old texts, and of course there will be
new topics to share.

All these articles, more than 900 of them, have been an excuse for me to
learn new things. Writing them is a pleasure for me, and I hope
reading them has been enjoyable for you. In the end one writes for
others, and I have to thank you for your patience and your dedication to my
texts. I have always tried to give you an interesting experience, one in
which we share some new knowledge. Forgive the flaws. I try to
get better with each new article, but I suspect I still need many more
flight hours, and more patience for proofreading :wink:.

Thanks for visiting, you will always be welcome. We are starting a new
year. Let's see what La Naturaleza Del Software brings us.
