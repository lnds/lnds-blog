The Department of Computer Science of the Universidad de
Chile and its alumni community, with the support of INTEL Chile, invite you to
take part in the first discussion panel **\"Cloud Computing in Chile:
a conceptual framework for the cloud\".**

*What defines a Cloud Computing solution?*

*What characteristics do the offerings of local providers have?*

*Are there actually offerings in the local market with the
characteristics proper to Cloud Computing?*

*What has the experience of implementing cloud services been like
in the organizations that have developed this kind of solution?*

*What should we look at before starting a project to adopt
the cloud?*

These are some of the topics that will be reviewed with the guests at the
discussion table.

In 1969, Leonard Kleinrock, one of the chief scientists of
ARPANET, the project that gave rise to the Internet, said: \"As of now,
computer networks are still in their infancy, but as
they grow up and become more sophisticated, we will probably
see the spread of 'computer utilities,' which like
present electric and telephone utilities, will service
individual homes and offices across the country.\"

This vision of computing services, based on a
utility model, anticipates the massive transformation of the entire
computing industry, in which computing services
will be available on demand, like water,
electricity, gas and telephone services in today's society.

In the context of this challenge, this first discussion panel is being held
to review how far this trend has developed in Chile.

The Department of Computer Science of the Universidad de
Chile and its alumni community, with the support of INTEL Chile, invite you to
take part in the first discussion panel Cloud Computing in Chile:
