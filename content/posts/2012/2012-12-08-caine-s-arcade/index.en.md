I found out about this wonderful story [via Pensamientos Ágiles](http://brigomp.blogspot.com/2012/04/el-salon-recreativo-de-caine.html?utm_source=feedburner&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=Feed%3A+Pensamientosgiles+%28Pensamientos+%C3%A1giles%29).

They say a child needs only his imagination, and maybe a cardboard
box, to be happy, and in this case Caine shows us that this gift
can bring joy to a lot of people.

{% vimeo 40000072 %}

The story appeared on Reddit and had a big impact, as you can see
in the video. There is currently an initiative to fund a scholarship
for Caine and other kids as imaginative as he is, which so far has
raised more than 200,000 dollars: [](http://cainesarcade.com/)
