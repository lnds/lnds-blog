Unfortunately the stupidity of anti-vaccination is showing up in
our country, and the worst part is that it gets coverage on broadcast TV,
with all the consequences that brings: the spread of a lie.
Vaccines do not cause autism!

But Penn & Teller explain it better:

{{<youtube SH72ONzA9c8>}}

Vaccines do not cause autism; there is no evidence proving any kind of
relationship between thimerosal and autism. It is all based on a
fraudulent study carried out by [Andrew
Wakefield.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Wakefield)

And yet this myth keeps spreading over the internet,
even though a series of studies have been conducted that ruled out
any kind of relationship between autism and vaccines.

Penn & Teller's question is the relevant one: which side do you want
your child on? On the side of those who don't get vaccinated and can end up crippled
by polio, or die of diphtheria? Vaccinate your child. Vaccines
do not cause autism.

Ignorance kills.
