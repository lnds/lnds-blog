![](los-supersonicos1.jpg) The
Jetsons turned 50. It was on September 23, 1962 that the
first episode of the futuristic animated series aired, on
the ABC network.

The surprising thing is that The Jetsons only aired for one
season; they were a television flop in their day. Until 1985 there
were only 24 episodes of The Jetsons!
