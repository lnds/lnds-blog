Over the years I've come to understand that when I've let go, when I've stopped chasing ambitious plans, when I stop desperately searching for something, that's when I get what I really need and want.

I've learned that if you give, you get much more back. It sounds like I'm writing one of those motivational spiels people post on their Facebook or Instagram walls, but it's what I feel right now.

When I realized you can't be happy, happiness came into my life. When I discovered that the pursuit of power only brings misery and destruction, I left that road and was able to find myself and discover who was really with me.

So far I have a full life. I do what I like, always alongside brilliant people who inspire me. I also have a wonderful family that makes me proud. I don't have to go around showing or proving anything to anyone. I'm recognized for who I am, and that's with the most exciting thing I post on Instagram being pictures of lizards.

Today I turn 55, and sometimes I feel like I'm barely 35. I've gotten younger. Of course, sometimes my body doesn't get the memo, but that's a detail.

My gift to you this year is a great Steely Dan song, one that says in one of its verses:

> They got a name for the winners in the world\
> I want a name when I lose

See you on the next trip around the sun.
