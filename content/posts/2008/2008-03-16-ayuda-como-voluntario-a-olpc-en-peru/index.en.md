While Uruguay [announces the purchase of 200,000](http://www.elpais.com.uy/08/03/15/pnacio_335673.asp) more laptops for the CEIBAL project, in Peru the group [FuenteLibre.org](http://www.fuentelibre.org/) is inviting anyone who wants to volunteer to support the OLPC project in that country.

Since I know there are people in Peru who read this blog, let me point out to anyone interested that you can sign up as a volunteer by following these steps:

1. Create a new volunteer ticket at:  
[http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/newticket/](http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/newticket/)

2. Send an email with some information about yourself, where you are from, what you do, and so on, plus a phone number where you can be reached, to:  
voluntario@laptop.org

3. Get an OpenID account so you can add content to the site:  
[http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/wiki/OpenID](http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/wiki/OpenID)

4. Add yourself to the list of volunteers at:  
[http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/wiki/QuienesSomos](http://meta.fuentelibre.org/trac/wiki/QuienesSomos)
