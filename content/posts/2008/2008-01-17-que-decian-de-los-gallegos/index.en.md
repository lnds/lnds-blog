It turns out that, building on [the ideas of Johnny Chung Lee](/en/blog/lnds/2007/12/24/the-magic-of-innovating/), a group of [Galician teachers built an electronic whiteboard](http://centros.edu.xunta.es/ceipdepalmeira/rato_pizarra.htm) using a Wii remote and a projector. As shown in the video:

{{<youtube -R0cms89gJs>}}

The instructions for pulling this off [are here](http://centros.edu.xunta.es/ceipdepalmeira/rato_pizarra.htm) (in Galician).

This strikes me as a good deal cheaper than any similar technology available on the market.
