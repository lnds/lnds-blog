[William Gibson](http://www.williamgibsonbooks.com/), the famous science fiction writer, author of Neuromancer and of the concept of cyberspace, [has put it rather well](http://www.thestandard.com/article/0,1902,5466,00.html?page=0%2C1):

> "Clouds are [numinous](http://www.rae2.es/numinoso)," Gibson says. "But the cloud's main usefulness lies in its vagueness, like cyberspace - a word which is also useful for its vagueness. Or perhaps," he adds, "people are thinking of thought balloons in comics."
