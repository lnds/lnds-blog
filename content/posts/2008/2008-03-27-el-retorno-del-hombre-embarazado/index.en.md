A few days ago I noticed a jump in visits to this blog, and from the most unexpected source: a [High School Musical forum](http://www.hsm-rocks.com/foros/index.php)!

The reason: a kid posted the fake news story about a supposed pregnant man. The surprising part is that apparently some TV channel, in I do not know which country, aired [this news item](http://cdp.blogsome.com/2005/08/08/el-hombre-embarazado-de-emolcom/), again.

![hombreembarazado2008.jpg](hombreembarazado2008.jpg)

And since then the visits have gone up and there has been no shortage of [naive comments](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/08/the-pregnant-man-and-the-transgenic-mouse/#comment-56180). Even though I put a clarifying line at the top of the article, there are still people who read nothing, and some have gone as far as coming up with [strange theories](/en/blog/lnds/2005/08/08/the-pregnant-man-and-the-transgenic-mouse/#comment-56424sobre) about a woman who supposedly changed sex and is now pregnant (!?).

I demand an explanation!
