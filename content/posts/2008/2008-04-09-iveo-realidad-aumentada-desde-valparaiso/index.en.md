After reading the [note about a contest](http://www.mouse.cl/detail.asp?story=2008%2F04%2F08%2F17%2F36%2F44) organized by USM and Microsoft (which explains several things ;), I discovered this interesting project. It is called [IVEO](http://www.gled.cl/iveo.php), an augmented reality application. I invite you to watch this video to understand what it is about:

  





This is the first product from [GLED](http://www.gled.cl/), a company formed by young computer engineering students at the [Universidad Federico Santa María](http://www.usm.cl/). Very interesting work. Oh, and you can [download the software](http://www.gled.cl/iveo/iveo-1.01b-win32.exe) and the [cards](http://www.gled.cl/iveo/Album.pdf) to try it out, from the GLED site.
