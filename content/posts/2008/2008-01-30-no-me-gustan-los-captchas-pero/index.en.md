Regular readers of this blog know that [I don't like captchas](/en/blog/lnds/2005/10/02/reverse-turing-tests-and-accessibility/), but the truth is I'm fed up with spam comments. A good deal of time gets wasted managing them, and I'm tired of the whole thing.

So we are going to turn captchas on, using the Movable Type default, and I hope you don't get one as hideous as [this one](/2007/05/no_a_los_captcha.html).

Incidentally, I turned the captcha on by accident a few months ago and I think it worked, though I suspect too well, because comments dropped to nearly 0 :(

The truth is I think there are better options, but I don't have much time to implement them right now, so I hope you'll forgive me. And if anybody knows of an alternative that works with Movable Type 4, leave me a note (if the captcha lets you).
