This time I'm going to propose a challenge. I'm thinking mainly of young programmers who would like to take part in events like the [ONI](http://www.oni.cl/) (or the [IOI](http://www.fayerwayer.com/2008/07/olimpiadas-internacionales-de-informatica/)). If this has any success, I'll propose another challenge.

The challenge is the following:

Produce a zig-zag array.

A zig-zag array is a square array of the first N2 integers, where the numbers run from lowest to highest, laid out in a zig-zag along the anti-diagonals of the matrix (see the figure).

For example, if N is 5, the program must produce this array:

0   1   5   6  14
2   4   7 13  15
3   8 12 16  21
9 11 17 20  22
10 18 19 23  24

* Although this may look like a brain teaser, and a somewhat useless one, the truth is that this kind of array is used in the JPEG image compression algorithm.
