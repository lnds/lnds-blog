"Augmented reality consists of a set of devices that add virtual information to the physical information that already exists. This is the main difference from virtual reality, since it does not replace physical reality but overlays computer data on the real world." ([according to Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality))

Yesterday I showed you the work of [GLED](http://www.gled.cl/) and its product [IVEO](http://www.gled.cl/iveo.php).
