This is a quick note about several things:

* What [good news](http://www.neutralidadsi.org/2008/03/31/proyecto-de-neutralidad-aprobado-5-a-0-en-la-comision-del-senado/)
about the technological neutrality bill being approved in the
Senate's transport and telecommunications committee. That does not
mean it is law yet.

* I do not know if it happens to you, but this kind of news always throws me off,
because it is badly reported. You are always left wondering: "did
law X get passed?", "but wait, hadn't they passed it already?", and the
worst part is that when the law is finally enacted, nobody reports it! (or
it gets reduced to a note in a corner). It also happens that a law
that was taken as passed ends up rejected, or goes to a joint committee,
or ends up as something completely different. Oh well.

  * "Tugar.cl":http://www.tugar.cl/ is a spectacular application!
Well, I really like what I have seen and the potential it has.
Congratulations to its [creators](http://bootlog.cl/), that
is innovation. Innovation does not mean creating something new or totally
original. Many times, by integrating two or more features that
already exist you can create a new product, a composition of things, and the
result is greater than the sum of its parts ([via Huasonic](http://www.huasonic.com/2008/03/31/de-webprendedor-tugar/)).

  * This blog has its [spot on Twitter](http://twitter.com/lnds),
and has for a while actually, but I had not told you
officially. Do not expect great things. It is almost always random
ramblings, some link I stumble on, and I post very
rarely, but I think several of you who read the blog did not know about
it.
