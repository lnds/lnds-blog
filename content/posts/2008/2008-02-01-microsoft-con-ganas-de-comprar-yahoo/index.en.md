According to [an official Microsoft press release](http://www.microsoft.com/presspass/press/2008/feb08/02-01CorpNewsPR), they are making an offer to buy Yahoo for a total of 46 billion dollars, at 31 dollars per share (that would be 62 percent above the current share price).
