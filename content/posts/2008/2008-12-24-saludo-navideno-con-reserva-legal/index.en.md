Merry Christmas to all of you!

> Legal Disclaimer
>
> Please accept my wishes without this implying any obligation or
> commitment whatsoever, implicit or explicit.\
> The Merry Christmas wishes I am sending do not imply, nor should they
> be understood as, religious proselytizing, each of the recipients of
> this greeting being absolutely free to enjoy said date under the
> practices and traditions of the religion they profess, or under the
> secular practices they wish, or under the religion or secular
> practices of others or, at their option, to engage in no religious or
> secular practice whatsoever.\
> The wishes for happiness and prosperity for the New Year are made
> assuming the calendar generally accepted in the Western hemisphere,
> but do not imply ignorance of the existence of other calendars or of
> the cultures that sustain them, which since pre-Hispanic times have
> made our country great (which does not necessarily imply that our
> country is greater than other countries in the region or in other
> parts of the world, which I say particularly for our Argentine,
> Bolivian, North American and Norwegian colleagues.\
> Said countries have been listed in alphabetical order, which does not
> imply any preference or animosity. The expression North American does
> not entail ignorance of the several countries in North America).\
> This greeting is made without distinction, exclusion or preference
> based on race, color, sex, age, marital status, union membership,
> religion, political opinion, nationality, national extraction or
> social origin, or any other consideration that could be understood as
> offensive or discriminatory.\
> By accepting this greeting, its terms shall also be accepted,
> indivisibly.\
> In any case, this greeting is subject to clarification or even
> withdrawal ipso iure by the undersigned, in whole or in part, without
> any prior notification or notice.\
> Likewise, this greeting is freely transferable, provided its original
> text is not altered and, in any case, without any further liability
> for the undersigned.\
> In the same way, this greeting implies no commitment or obligation
> whatsoever on the part of the undersigned to implement or assist in
> the fulfillment of the wishes each person may have, nor may it be
> understood as a form of participation, inducement or concealment,
> should anyone have wishes that are contrary to or prohibited by law
> and these come to pass.\
> This greeting shall have a term of one year and shall not be
> renewable, and shall therefore expire by operation of law on
> Christmas of the year 2009.\
> Likewise, it implies no guarantee whatsoever that the wishes and
> congratulations will actually come true, it being made clear that
> past happiness fluctuates, so nothing guarantees it will be repeated
> in the future.\
> It is emphasized that this Christmas greeting is granted out of mere
> liberality and does not constitute a precedent obliging its
> repetition in future years, whatever the circumstances in which the
> greeter and the greetee may find themselves. It should likewise be
> noted that any greetings that may be extended during the year, for
> birthdays or saints' days, are made with no intent to novate the
> Christmas greeting, which remains unaltered throughout the Roman
> calendar year or whichever may replace it in the future.
