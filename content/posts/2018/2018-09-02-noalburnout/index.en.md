Working long hours, working overtime, even when they try to sell it to
you as something "cool" and innovative, is neither. It is not agile and
it is not transformation; it is the exact opposite of lean.

Burnout \[1\] is the physical, mental or emotional exhaustion caused by
overexertion or stress. But it is more than being stressed or
overworked. This phenomenon makes the things we once loved about our
work or our life lose their meaning. It generally shows up as a feeling
that there is no support, no help to get out of that situation.

Studies show that burnout can be almost as harmful to workers' health
as smoking or obesity. Its symptoms include feeling exhausted and
cynical about work; you are no longer motivated by your job and you
couldn't care less what happens to it. Or in other cases you feel
ineffective, unable to accomplish anything.

When you feel you are getting nothing done at work, or when your work
negatively affects other aspects of your life, you are suffering from
burnout. The bad part is that in extreme cases this can lead to
depression and even suicide.


## Causes of burnout

Research carried out at the University of California, Berkeley found
six organizational risks that can produce burnout\[2\]:

1.  Work overload: tasks that exceed the limits of a human being.
2.  Lack of control: the inability to influence the decisions that
    affect your work.
3.  Insufficient rewards: whether financial, institutional or social.
4.  Breakdown of community: a work environment lacking in solidarity.
5.  Absence of fairness: a lack of fairness in decision-making
    processes.
6.  Conflicting values: a mismatch between the organization's values
    and the individual's values.

## How can you detect it?


In their book "Accelerate"\[3\], the authors explain that they measured
the degree of burnout with the following questions:


-   Do you feel fatigued or exhausted by your work?
-   Do you feel indifferent or cynical about your work? Or do you feel
    you are not effective?
-   Is your work having a negative effect on your life?


## What to do about it?

Burnout can be prevented, and it is the leaders' responsibility to keep
it in check.


In IT, we can achieve this by fostering a work environment that
supports employees. We must make sure the work is meaningful, and that
those who carry it out understand how their job ties into meeting the
organization's strategic objectives.


The big problem with leadership is that it usually focuses on changing
people while ignoring the work environment, when it is well known that
a change in the environment produces vital, longer-lasting success.


To avoid burnout, according to \[3\], we should focus on:

-   Fostering a respectful, supportive work environment. An environment
    that emphasizes learning from mistakes rather than blame for them.
-   Communicating a strong sense of purpose.
-   Investing in employee development.
-   Asking employees what is keeping them from achieving their goals
    and then fixing those things.
-   Giving employees time, space and resources to experiment and learn.
-   Finally, they must be given the authority to make decisions that
    affect their work and their role, particularly in areas where they
    are responsible for the outcomes.
