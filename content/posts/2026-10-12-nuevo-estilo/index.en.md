The blog has a new look, and there's something else new: from now on the posts will be in English too. I'm translating the back catalog with the help of AI, so my apologies in advance.

This is how I write these days: I write the draft entirely myself, with no help from AI. I use AI to research, to fix grammar and spelling, and to translate.

I'll be reviewing the translations too, but they will be mostly machine-generated text, so bear with me.
You can always open a PR against the blog's repo to propose changes, or open an issue.
The address is: <https://github.com/lnds/lnds-blog>

Since there are so many posts, by the time this one goes out only the articles
from August 1, 2025 onward will be translated; I'll get through the rest over time.

I hope you like it. Several posts are coming about the projects and courses I'm working on, so stay tuned.
