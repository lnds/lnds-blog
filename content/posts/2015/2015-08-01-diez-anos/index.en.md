This blog turns ten today. Some say blogs were born in
2005 and died in 2006 with the arrival of Facebook and the social
media boom.

The truth is that by 2008 many Chilean bloggers had already
quit. In Chile, out of the *"Cambrian blogging explosion"* of 2005,
and in particular in the *phylum* of personal
tech blogs, I think the only survivors are
[Alejandro Barros](//www.alejandrobarros.com/una-decada-de-mi-blog),
whose blog turned
ten in June, and me.
Although I admit
that Alejandro has kept up a more regular publishing schedule.

Right now this site has 948 published posts. My
books, ["La Naturaleza Del Software"](//amzn.to/1OGRVYw),
["Los Enigmas de Turing"](//amzn.to/1OGRWM2) and
["Akarrú"](//amzn.to/1gxLCMe), add up to more than 850 copies sold on
[Amazon](//www.amazon.com/Eduardo-D%C3%ADaz/e/B00GFZWPPC/ref=sr_ntt_srch_lnk_2?qid=1438442928&sr=8-2),
which makes me very happy. To all of you who have bought my books,
I want to say thank you for choosing
them.

And of course thanks to all of you who keep visiting this
blog.

## Evolution

530 million years ago, in the Cambrian period, there was an
explosion of life in which countless kinds of species appeared that,
if they were alive today, would look very strange to us. But in one of those
mass extinction events that have ravaged our planet, more than 90%
of those species disappeared.

A lot has changed since 2005, when we had our Cambrian period in
blogging. Today very few of us are still blogging,
running a personal blog. Some blogs turned into professional
media outlets, but the vast majority disappeared.

I remember a lot of people would write a first post announcing their intention
to publish something every week; two weeks later they published a post
apologizing for not having published, and promising
posts again. The third post never came, of course.

Someone said that if your life was very interesting you didn't have
time to write a blog. That's a fallacy, of course. You can
have a very interesting life and blog.

Many of you post your ideas on Facebook, and when you do you are
blogging. Your comments on Twitter make you a
microblogger. Or if you post a video on YouTube you are video
blogging. Just as happened with life on this planet, blogs did not go
extinct, they just turned into new species. That is called
[evolution](/blog/lnds/2009/8/12/cuestion-de-evolucion).

As *"la Mercedes"* (Mercedes Sosa) sings, *everything changes, but my love
doesn't change, no matter how far away I am*. Although I don't write
as often as I used to, the urge to write is still there.

Again, thank you all. I hope we keep seeing each other for ten more years.
