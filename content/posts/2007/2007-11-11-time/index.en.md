It's my birthday. 41 years old, phew...

Twelve years ago, to celebrate my birthday, I went to this Alan Parsons concert in Santiago (where this video comes from). What remains of that show is this video of Time, a song that reminds us that the one thing we can't stop is the passage of time.

{{<youtube 22FnxgCNeKU>}}  


The great part is finding more Alan Parsons videos, a side effect of preparing a post. The cool thing about this version of Sirius is that it was the first time AP played live, and with many of the original members (except Eric Woolfson, of course), a gift for fans like me:
