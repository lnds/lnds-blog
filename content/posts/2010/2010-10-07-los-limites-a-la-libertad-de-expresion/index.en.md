> "In the end, freedom of expression is one of the foundations of the
> democratic system. With limited freedom of expression we get less public
> debate of ideas and less oversight of power. We can always come up with
> reasons to limit it. To protect the people from heresy, to
> protect our children, to protect the nation, the homeland or the
> race. Not long ago Jesus was even given a legal address at a law
> office on Paseo Ahumada in Santiago in order to block the screening of
> a film. **Freedom of expression means allowing the publication
> of precisely those things we don't like**. Faced with
> the problems that come with freedom of expression, the answer
> should be **more** freedom of expression, not limiting it."

[Claudio Ruiz at Quemar Las Naves](http://www.quemarlasnaves.net/2010/10/07/jesus-como-limite-a-la-libertad-de-expresion/).
I couldn't agree more.
