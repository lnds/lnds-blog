If you watch the film [Revolution OS](http://www.revolution-os.com/), there is a moment when Linus
Torvalds presents an award to the FSF, represented by Richard
Stallman.

On receiving the award, Stallman opens with a very ironic remark: that
this is like Han Solo giving an award to the Rebel Alliance. Then he
carries on with his usual speech while Linus plays with his daughters,
not paying much attention.

That scene is very revealing.

I have always wondered what would have become of the free software
movement without the Linux kernel. Likening Linus Torvalds to Han Solo
is no small comparison. Remember that if Han Solo hadn't shown up and
attacked Darth Vader's ship, poor Luke would never have pulled off his
feat.

On [Star Wars Day (or Luke Skywalker Day)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Day) I couldn't
let that go unmentioned.

May the 4th be with you.
