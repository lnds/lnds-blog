Taking ownership, of what you are and of what you have to do. That is
basic, and yet you see so little of it.

In education, my wife, who is a teacher, always complains
about those teachers who push away the "problem child", the restless
one, the one who is slow to learn, to the point of expelling him from
school instead of taking on the job of educating him and solving that
kid's problem.

That is what Fernando Paulsen highlights in [his column in Qué Pasa](http://www.quepasa.cl/articulo/8_3839_9.html), about
Mario Waissbluth's book. I ask you to read it. The anecdote he tells
is very enlightening:
[http://www.quepasa.cl/articulo/8*3839*9.html](http://www.quepasa.cl/articulo/8_3839_9.html)
