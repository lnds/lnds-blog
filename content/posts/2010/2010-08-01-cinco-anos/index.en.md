Today La Naturaleza del Software (LNDS) turns 5. In that time
I have written more than 900 entries, averaging 4 posts a week
(\*).

I have started this new year with a change of platform and look. Today
begins a month of celebration of our fifth anniversary. We will have
some guests and, for the first time, there will be at least one post
every day. We are going to recycle the best of what we have published
in this time.

I hope you enjoy these days as much as I will. If there is an article or
topic you would like us to revisit, I look forward to your comments. I
would also like to hear your opinions and suggestions for improvement.

Thank you for reading me these five years.

(\*) Unfortunately [the March disaster](/en/blog/lnds/2010/03/14/me-and-my-big-mouth/) made me
lose many of them, and I have not yet had the time to finish
recovering them all, but that work continues, so at some point
we will have all that information back.
