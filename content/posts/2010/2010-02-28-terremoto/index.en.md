The early hours of Saturday, **February** 27, 2010 were among the
most intense moments I've ever lived through. I've been through several
earthquakes already, mainly the one in 1985. This one felt much stronger to me, and
it seems it actually is. I'm only now getting connected to the internet;
until now all communication, and the few tweets, went through
movistar's 3G network, which, for all that I complain about its
service, has worked flawlessly this time, above all because it
let me get in touch immediately with my loved ones, and above all
with my wife, who is in the Coquimbo region right
now.\
I just hope to have the whole family together soon, so I can
rest easy.\
Thanks to those who have sent messages, and to my family and friends who have
asked through social networks. Greetings to everyone, we're
fine.

For more information about the earthquake, I recommend [reading this special on the 2010
earthquake at Manzana
Mecánica](http://www.manzanamecanica.org/2010/02/especial_terremoto_en_chile.html).
