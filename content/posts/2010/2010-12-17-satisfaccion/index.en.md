There are two important things you have to do in this life: give thanks
and give recognition.

Last night at work I had the chance to recognize several of my
collaborators, and I also received a recognition from my company, which
made me very happy.

For a couple of years we have taken on important projects. That is why
doing our job well matters so much, considering security and compliance
with quality standards and regulations; and of course trying to make as
few mistakes as possible, because if we got it wrong we would affect
millions of Chilean workers.

When it falls to you to lead a team, you have to take on that task with
great commitment and respect for your collaborators. I'm not telling you
anything new, but in spite of everything they teach you, and all the
coaching you get, there are moments when this task is not easy.

An executive's main job is not tracking the Gantt chart, or building
Excel spreadsheets full of cold numbers and indicators. No, an
executive's main task and concern is working with people. The
executive's mission is to be a facilitator, and that even includes an
emotional toll you would never suspect.
*"Good leadership is about people"*.

But I wanted to tell you that I am satisfied, that in spite of all the
difficulties we faced we pulled through, and we succeeded in
 very important challenges, and I wanted to share that satisfaction with
you. Thanks to all of you for reading and following this blog; I hope
that looking back you feel something similar.
