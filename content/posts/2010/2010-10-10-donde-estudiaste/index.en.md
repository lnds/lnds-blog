> "...And even the rich cry. The graduate of a British school in
> La Dehesa will tend to hide where he really comes from when, at
> the public university, he faces the kids from the Latino, the Rubén Darío or the San
> Juan, never mind the heavyweights from the Lastarria or the Nacional, who
> eat anyone alive. And he'll say he went to "a small school in
> Lo Barnechea" rather than own up to his roots in front of people who despise
> privilege. As must have happened with the MAPU crowd in the 60s,
> when they said they had gone to "a school run by priests" rather than
> admit they were from the Verbo Divino or Saint George."

Francisco Javier Díaz on that brand of discrimination we
Chileans practice, judging people by the school they went to: <http://blogs.elmercurio.com/revistasabado/2010/10/09/donde-estudiaste.asp>
