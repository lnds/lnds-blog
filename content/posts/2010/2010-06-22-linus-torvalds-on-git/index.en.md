It's been a while since I wrote more technical articles, so I'm going to
pick up some topics I'd like to dig into (which is an excuse to learn
new things). And one topic that has interested me, for a couple of years
at least, is the development of DVCSs, distributed version control
systems. On this subject I'm more or less convinced that Git is the way
to go, but before explaining why, I want to leave you with this video of
Linus Torvalds talking about this DVCS. The video is also very
interesting because you rarely get to see Linus presenting, and
expressing his opinions live with as much force as he does in writing.

Here is the video. It's in English, with no subtitles, but if you are
going to make a living developing software I assume you know English,
and can listen to a talk in English without translators, right?
