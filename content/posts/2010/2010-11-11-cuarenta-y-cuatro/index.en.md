It's my 44th birthday. I think I'll remember this 2010, and I suppose many of
you will too, as a very special year, hectic and full of
emotions that took us along like a roller coaster.

But I'm happy to have completed another trip around the sun, and
thankful to everyone who reads me, and to those who have joined
this space recently.

of [Eric Woolfson](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Woolfson) playing
