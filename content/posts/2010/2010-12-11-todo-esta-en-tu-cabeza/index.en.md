Do you think Apple makes the best computers in the world?

Wrong. There is nothing special about them. I think they're bad, but
I'm not right either. It is a matter of _cultural implantation in our
brain_; _brand positioning_, the marketing people call it.

And there is scientific proof, [entertainingly explained](http://terceracultura.cl/2010/12/pepsi-challenge-2-0/) on
that wonderful blog called [Tercera Cultura](http://terceracultura.cl/), where they go over the results of an
experiment to measure this brand positioning thing:

> "The experiment was a recreation of the Pepsi Challenge, except this
> time all the tasting was being done in a brain scanner. **Each person
> swallowed sips of cola from a plastic tube while their brain was being
> scanned**. When Coke and Pepsi were offered unlabeled, the subjects
> showed no measurable preference for either brand. Most of the time,
> they couldn't even tell the two colas apart. But Montague's second
> observation was more surprising: **subjects overwhelmingly preferred
> drinks that were labeled as Coke, no matter what cola was actually
> delivered through the tubes**. In other words, **brand trumped
> taste**. **We cared more about the logo than the actual product**."

Go read [Tercera Cultura](http://terceracultura.cl/), you'll love it,
and while we're at it we might manage to position that brand ;)
