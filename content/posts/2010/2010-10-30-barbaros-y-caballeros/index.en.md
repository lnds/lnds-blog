The English have a saying: *"rugby is a game for
barbarians played by gentlemen, while football is a
game for gentlemen played by barbarians"*.

When it comes to programming languages, it seems to me that Java is like
rugby, and C\# is the equivalent of football.

For their part, the English who play cricket claim that _"cricket
is a game for gentlemen played by gentlemen"_.

So which one is the cricket of programming languages?

When the world was younger I would have answered: Delphi (Object
Pascal) is the cricket of programming languages! But now? I don't
know. Python? It has a long way to go. Scala? Too complicated. Haskell, or
F\#? Could be. I don't know, what do you think?
