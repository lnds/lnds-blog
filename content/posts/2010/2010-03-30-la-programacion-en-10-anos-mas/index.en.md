[According to Bertrand Meyer](http://bertrandmeyer.com/2010/03/29/verification-as-a-matter-of-course/),
ten years from now:

1.  We will still be using object-oriented languages
2.  Professional programming will be much more rigorous
3.  Verification will be built into the development process
4.  Every program will have a web interface
5.  Concurrency will be everywhere
6.  We will have more confidence in the assessment of objectives
7.  Software engineering will be not just a process, but a
    technology

Taken from Bertrand Meyer's presentation
["How you will be programming in 10 years"](http://se.ethz.ch/~meyer/publications/methodology/programming-10years-sierre.pdf)
