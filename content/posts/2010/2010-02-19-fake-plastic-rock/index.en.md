When I was a teenager I played drums in a band we put together with my
school friends. It was a beautiful time. We spent whole days
practicing in my room, which was conveniently far from the rest
of the house :wink:

Regular readers of this blog must find it odd that
several days have gone by without a post. The reason is quite simple: vacation.
But I'm back, although I plan to spend the last days of my vacation
with my kids forming a new band, though this time it's a plastic
band, with fake instruments and canned music in the form of a
video game, because I've caught the **Rock** Band 2 bug and I'm
playing like a man possessed.

To those of you who are parents, and who have the chance, I suggest you
buy this video game and share a good stretch of family
fun, enjoying good **rock**, of course.

Some comments:

If you've played drums you can start on medium or expert level
quickly, but of course it's not the same as playing the drums; the
layout isn't the same, and you only have one pedal. I suggest looking for a
drum kit with cymbals; with that you can set it up more like a
real kit.

It's cheaper to buy the instruments separately and the game on its own than
to buy the complete **rock** band game. In our case, we saved
about 40% of the cost.

And yes, it's very addictive and fun.

Of course I found out why my blog mentor [Jeff Attwood](http://www.codinghorror.com/blog/) is also a fan of
**Rock** Band, and the title of this post comes from
[his blog dedicated to this game](http://www.fakeplasticrock.com/).
