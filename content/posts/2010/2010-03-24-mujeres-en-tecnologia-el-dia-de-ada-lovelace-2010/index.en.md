March 24 is Ada Lovelace Day, which i[s a day
for blogging about the contribution of women in
technology](http://findingada.com/about/).

The spirit of Ada Lovelace Day is to promote and recognize the
outstanding work of our female colleagues in the field.

Last year my tribute went to [María Cecilia Rivara](/en/blog/lnds/2009/03/24/women-in-tech-ada-lovelace-day/).

This year my recognition goes to **Marcela Larenas and Paulina Lopez**,
and through them to a special group of talented colleagues who work
at REUNA.

{{<figure caption="Marcela Cárdenas, Paulina López and Marcela Larenas" src="equipo_artiproyectos.jpg">}}


Of the places where technology gets done in Chile, there are few where
women have a leadership role, but REUNA is a very notable case.
I've had the chance to work with Marcela and her team, and they are
top-notch.

May this day serve to remind us of the many colleagues of ours who
work in technology, to salute them, and to encourage more
women to follow this path.

And I take the chance to salute all the women who contribute to technology,
and in particular my fellow computer engineers. Happy Ada Lovelace Day.
