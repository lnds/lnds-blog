> Every morning, when I wake again under the pall of the sky, I feel
> that for me it is New Year's Day. That is why I hate these New Year's
> Days with a fixed date, which turn life and the human spirit into a
> commercial concern, with its consumption and its balance sheet and
> its forecast of expenses and income for the old and new management.
> \-- ***Gramsci***

I am writing this from one of my favorite spots in my house, surrounded
by trees and plants, on New Year's morning, two thousand nineteen.

Two thousand eighteen, what a year! Yesterday I read on social media
how several acquaintances were begging for the "shitty year" to be
over, and by one of those strange, ironic coincidences, on my phone's
shuffle playlist I heard a song by Ervin Drake, performed by Frank
Sinatra, titled "It Was a Very Good Year" (a romantic song that looks
back on old loves).


Last year had a bit of everything for me: valuable, beloved people left
us, and I saw friends go through a rough time, others improve their
lives, and some achieve great success. Personally I had some very hard
and stressful moments, and others that were tremendously comforting
and happy. Toward the end, several achievements, closures and
successful results. But, as a slogan from the industry I work in goes,
"past performance is no guarantee of future results".

## Moving forward

On the one hand, I agree with what Gramsci says in his ["Odio il
Capodanno"](http://izquierdadiario.com/Odio-el-ano-nuevo-de-Antonio-Gramsci?fbclid=IwAR12yXn12BfJ6TpT-2XGt_fOPzMIKbPWmFNLBGd8i8XJNExFPp8PhZnjXqA):
every morning we should feel that it is New Year's Day. That invites us
not to give up, to keep fighting for our goals, wishes and aspirations.

In The Ghost Rider, Neil Peart writes:

> * Pack up all those phantoms\
> Shoulder that invisible load\
> Keep on riding north and west\
> Haunting that wilderness road\
> Like a ghost rider\
> Keep on riding north and west\
> Then circle south and east\
> Show me beauty, but there is no peace\
> For the ghost rider*

And he means that we should take our past like a backpack and move
forward in our life, however we can, even with those ghosts on our
backs.

But let's move forward, because if we move forward the shadows begin
to disappear, as we free ourselves from the dead weight life leaves us
with:

> *There's a shadow on the road behind\
> There's a shadow on the road ahead\
> Nothing can stop you now\
> \
>  Sunrise in the mirror\
> * *Lightens that invisible load\
> * *Riding on a nameless quest\
> * *Haunting that wilderness road\
> \
> Like a ghost rider\
> \
>  Just an escape artist\
> * *Racing against the night\
> * *A wandering hermit\
> * *Racing toward the light*

We move toward the light, whatever that means for each one of us.

Happy New Year, friends. Don't stop moving forward.
