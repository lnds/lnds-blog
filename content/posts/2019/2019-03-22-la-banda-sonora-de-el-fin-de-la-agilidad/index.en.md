Thanks to everyone who has sent me comments and liked the last three
posts, a series I've titled "The End of Agile".

It's the most personal series I've ever written, and to write it I've
gone back to the musical moments of the years the story covers. It
isn't necessarily the music that was in fashion at the time. Sometimes
it's the music I was listening to back then, sometimes it was music
that was in fashion, sometimes it's music that reflects how I felt in
those periods, or it may simply be that the songs have some connection.

I'm someone who loves music deeply, the son of a radio man (something
I [wrote about elsewhere](/en/blog/lnds/2015/06/21/the-spirit-of-radio/)).
So I've decided to make this a "multimedia experience", and from now
on each post will come with a soundtrack: a mini playlist on Spotify
with a sample of about 10 representative songs or pieces.

For those who have read the series so far, here are the links to the
